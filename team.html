Our Leadership
Founders & Directors
Mr. G.M. Patel
Founder & Chairman
A visionary entrepreneur and first-generation real estate developer, G.M. Patel founded Suryam Group in 1993, establishing a legacy of transforming Ahmedabad's urban landscape.

With a keen understanding of market dynamics and a commitment to exceeding customer expectations, he has guided Suryam's strategic growth from a local enterprise to a prominent force in Gujarat's real estate sector. His leadership is characterised by a focus on long-term vision, fostering a corporate culture of integrity and excellence, and building enduring relationships with clients and partners. Mr. Patel's foresight has been instrumental in positioning Suryam as a leader in creating exceptional living environments that redefine luxury.
Ishan Patel
Director — Branding & Communication
As Director of Design at Suryam Group, Ishan provides the visionary leadership that defines the aesthetic identity of each development.

An architect by training, he blends a deep understanding of spatial design, materiality, and modern lifestyles with a refined aesthetic sensibility and meticulous attention to detail. He champions a collaborative design process, prioritising client aspirations to create bespoke living environments. Working closely with the design team, consultants, and clients, he ensures every detail is carefully considered and executed to the highest standards. From concept to completion, Ishan guides the design process, ensuring each development reflects Suryam's commitment to creating elegant, sophisticated, and timeless living spaces.
Ms. Sweta Patel
Director, CSR & Landscape
Sweta Patel plays a vital role in shaping Suryam's commitment to both social responsibility and environmental stewardship. She directs the company's corporate social responsibility initiatives, developing and implementing programmes that create positive impact within the community. Furthermore, she oversees the landscape architecture of all Suryam projects, ensuring that each development seamlessly integrates with its natural surroundings. Her focus is on creating aesthetically refined and ecologically conscious spaces that enhance the quality of life for residents and contribute to a more sustainable future.
Mr. Hitesh Patel
Director, Operations
A highly accomplished engineer with a distinguished academic background from the National Institute of Technology, Hitesh Patel leads Suryam's operational strategy, ensuring seamless execution of complex development projects. He oversees all aspects of project management, from planning and procurement to construction and delivery. His expertise lies in optimising processes, managing budgets effectively, and maintaining rigorous quality control standards. Hitesh's focus on precision and efficiency ensures the timely delivery of exceptional quality in every Suryam development, reinforcing the company's reputation for reliability and excellence.
Aajal Patel
Director — Sales
With over two decades of experience navigating Ahmedabad's dynamic real estate market, Aajal directs Suryam's sales division, driving revenue growth and expanding market share. He possesses a deep understanding of market trends, consumer behaviour, and competitive dynamics. He excels at developing and implementing sophisticated sales strategies, cultivating key client relationships, and leading a high-performing sales team. Ajal's expertise has been pivotal in securing Suryam's position as a market leader and establishing strong connections with discerning clientele.
