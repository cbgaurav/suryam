A Forest Sanctuary in Ahmedabad
The Sage by Repose is an exclusive collection of courtyard villas set within a forest clearing on the edge of Ahmedabad. Designed around the principle that the finest address gives the most land back to the landscape, this development offers a rare connection between luxury living and the natural world.
Every villa has been positioned to maximise its relationship with the forest canopy, the birdsong, and the light that filters through the trees at different hours of the day.
Where Every Detail is Considered
Each villa at The Sage has been meticulously designed to honour the forest setting it inhabits. Courtyard plans that draw the outdoors in. Materials selected to age gracefully alongside the trees. An architecture that belongs to its place.
Choose from 3 and 4 bedroom courtyard configurations, each with private garden terraces, meditation courtyards, and forest views from every principal room.

The Sage Clubhouse
A sanctuary within a sanctuary. The Sage Clubhouse offers residents a space for connection, rejuvenation, and celebration. From the forest-view lounge to the resort-style pool, every detail has been crafted to enhance the quality of life.
Here, the community comes together — for morning swim sessions, evening gatherings, and the quiet pleasure of a shared landscape that keeps revealing new things.
