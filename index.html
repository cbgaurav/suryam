Suryam Arnath
There is a kind of developer who builds to the brief. And a kind who rewrites it.
Since 1993, Suryam has belonged to the second category ‚bringing together ecologists, water engineers, botanists, and international architects around each project before a single wall is drawn, then asking a question most developers never think to ask: what does this land want to become?
The answer has taken different forms across three decades. Nature communities of 150 acres. A private waterfront estate arranged around a living, engineered lake. Courtyard villas in a forest clearing. An urban address that has quietly renewed one of Ahmedabad's most storied neighbourhoods. But the method has never changed: start with the land, follow its lead, and build something that the site itself would have asked for.
This discipline is what more than 4,000 families have chosen to live inside. It is what we are still building.
