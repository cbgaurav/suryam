Pavilion of Light, Wind, and Water
Welcome to Ahmedabad’s most exclusive waterfront estate; a low-density, 100-acre sanctuary where life flows at the pace of nature.
By The Waters is an uncompromising world of just 99 limited-edition private domains. Inspired by the eternal connection between humanity and water, this masterwork offers an unparalleled living experience where elemental nature and architectural artistry coexist in perfect harmony.
The Architecture of Stillness
Every villa at By The Waters is a dedicated sanctuary for the senses, masterfully envisioned by SCDA Singapore. Expansive floor-to-ceiling windows invite the stillness of the water indoors, complemented by refined finishes and select interiors that embody pure restraint.
Built on a scale that gracefully accommodates private pools, landscaped gardens, and direct waterfront access, each home is a considered, unique response to its natural site.

The Water's Edge
A conscious structural anchor within the larger landscape, built for physical connection and quiet restoration. From the lakefront lounge to the swimming pool, every detail is crafted to interface directly with the water.
This is where the community footprint takes shape for morning laps, evening gatherings, and the shared experience of a terrain that quietly changes with the elements.
A Ledger of Landscape and Leisure
A deliberate, balanced ecosystem of indoor and outdoor amenities mapped along the water’s edge. From the structured luxury of the spa, pool, and yoga decks to natural anchor points like the Kayak Club, Shunya Forest, and Barefoot Island; these spaces are skillfully orchestrated to offer a deeply grounded, unhurried way of living.
